Our Story & Leadership

About Hills of Glory

Called by God to raise a glorious generation, preach the uncompromised Gospel, and manifest God's love to all nations.

Purpose

Our Mission

To proclaim the Gospel of Jesus Christ with power, equip believers for effective ministry, build disciples across generations, and impact cities with the tangible love and grace of God.

Looking Forward

Our Vision

To be a vibrant, multi-campus house of prayer for all nations, raising up leaders and families who honor Jesus Christ and transform communities globally.

Foundations

Core Values

Passionate Worship, Biblical Authority, Unwavering Faith, Sacrificial Love, Holy Spirit Empowerment, and Generous Community Service.

What We Believe

Statement of Belief

The unchanging truths upon which our faith and ministry are established.

The Holy Scriptures

We believe the Bible is the inspired, infallible, and authoritative Word of God, serving as the supreme rule for all faith and Christian practice.

The Holy Trinity

We believe in one eternal God, existing in three co-equal persons: the Father, the Son (Jesus Christ), and the Holy Spirit.

Salvation Through Christ

We believe salvation is a free gift received through repentance and faith in Jesus Christ's shed blood, victorious resurrection, and ascension.

The Holy Spirit

We believe in the baptism and ongoing infilling of the Holy Spirit, empowering every believer with spiritual gifts to witness and bear fruit.
