About HLA

A Transformative Leadership Journey

Hills Leadership Academy is more than a training program — it's a life-changing experience that equips believers with the knowledge, character, and competencies to lead with confidence in ministry and the marketplace.

Program Information

Duration

12-Month Program

Full-time intensive training with evening and weekend classes to accommodate working students. Program runs annually from January to December.

Eligibility

Open to All Believers

New believers, mature Christians seeking leadership training, and anyone with a sincere desire to serve God and develop leadership capacity.

Investment

Tuition & Fees

Affordable tuition with payment plans available. Scholarships offered for students with demonstrated financial need. Contact us for details.

Completion

Certificate of Completion

Graduates receive an official HLA Certificate and recognition during a special graduation ceremony with family and church community.
