Programs

Discipleship Pathways

A clear, sequential journey designed to meet you where you are and guide you from your first steps of faith to graduation as a commissioned leader.

Journey
  1. 1
    Start Here

    Startup Sessions

    Welcome, new believer. Foundational talks on salvation, identity in Christ, and life in the church.

  2. 2
    NLR

    New Life Retreat

    A weekend immersive experience to encounter God, hear your story redeemed, and connect with community.

  3. 3
    DC

    Discipleship Course

    Structured teaching that grounds you in Scripture, prayer, and the daily habits of faith.

  4. 4
    FS

    Foundational Study

    Deeper theological study and spiritual formation that prepare your heart for leadership.

  5. Capstone

    Graduation

    Commissioned and released to serve — your journey becomes someone else's invitation.

Optional Extra Course

Leadership Essentials

An optional course for learners who want to grow their influence — open to join alongside or after your pathway, ready whenever you are.
