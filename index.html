PURPOSE

God's Purpose

From the very beginning, God has had a specific and beautiful purpose for your life. He is able to transform who you are and shape you into the person He designed you to become.

  1. 01

    Known and Chosen

    Before you were formed, God knew you and set you apart. You are not an accident — you were lovingly and intentionally created for a divine assignment.

  2. 02

    Created to Glorify God

    Every life is designed to reflect the goodness and glory of God. As you walk with Him, your life becomes a testimony of His faithfulness and love.

  3. 03

    Called to Transformation

    God does not leave you as you are. Through His Spirit, He renews your mind and transforms your character, making you whole and thriving in every area of life.

  4. 04

    Empowered to Serve

    God equips you with gifts and opportunities to serve others. Your purpose finds its richest expression when you pour out the love you have received.

  5. 05

    Given a Future and Hope

    God's plans for you are plans of peace and a future filled with hope. No matter your past, He is committed to completing the good work He began in you.

A New Beginning

Prayer of Acceptance

Begin your walk with Jesus through this simple prayer of faith — spoken from the heart, received by grace.

Lord Jesus, I come to You and acknowledge that I have sinned and fallen short of Your glory. Thank You for loving me and giving Your life for me. I believe You died and rose again so I could live. Today, I receive You as my Lord and Savior. Forgive my sins, cleanse my heart, and take Your rightful place in my life. Write my name in the Book of Life, and help me to live for You from this day forward.

Amen.
